Quan Troia cremava, Enees, fill de Venus i del troià Anquises, va fugir amb el seu pare a coll, el seu fill Ascani de la mà i els penats de la ciutat. Després d'anys de navegació, passant per Cartago, on la reina Dido es va matar quan ell la va abandonar, va arribar al Laci. Allà va vèncer el rei Turn i es va casar amb Lavínia. El seu fill va fundar Alba Longa, i del seu llinatge naixerien Ròmul i Rem. Per això la família Júlia, la de Cèsar i August, es deia descendent de Venus.
When Troy was burning, Aeneas, son of Venus and the Trojan Anchises, fled with his father on his shoulders, his son Ascanius by the hand and the city's household gods. After years at sea, by way of Carthage, where Queen Dido killed herself when he left her, he reached Latium. There he defeated King Turnus and married Lavinia. His son founded Alba Longa, and from his line Romulus and Remus would be born. That's why the Julian family, Caesar's and Augustus', claimed descent from Venus.

Amuli, rei d'Alba Longa, va obligar la seva neboda Rea Sílvia a fer-se vestal perquè no tingués fills. Però Mart la va fer mare de dos bessons. Amuli va manar llançar-los al Tíber; la cistella va encallar sota una figuera, al costat del Palatí, i una lloba els va alletar fins que els va recollir el pastor Fàustul. Ja grans, van restituir el seu avi al tron i van decidir fundar una ciutat. Van discutir on: Ròmul va veure dotze voltors des del Palatí; Rem, sis des de l'Aventí. Ròmul va traçar el solc de la muralla i, quan Rem el va saltar burlant-se'n, el va matar. Era el 21 d'abril del 753 aC, segons el càlcul de Varró.
Amulius, king of Alba Longa, forced his niece Rhea Silvia to become a Vestal so she would have no children. But Mars made her the mother of twins. Amulius ordered them thrown into the Tiber; the basket ran aground under a fig tree by the Palatine, and a she-wolf suckled them until the shepherd Faustulus took them in. Grown up, they restored their grandfather to the throne and decided to found a city. They argued over where: Romulus saw twelve vultures from the Palatine; Remus, six from the Aventine. Romulus ploughed the furrow for the wall and, when Remus jumped over it mockingly, killed him. It was 21 April 753 BC, by Varro's reckoning.

La nova ciutat de Ròmul tenia homes però poques dones, i els pobles veïns no volien casar les seves filles amb aquells fugitius. Ròmul va organitzar uns jocs en honor de Neptú i va convidar els sabins amb les seves famílies. A un senyal, els joves romans van raptar les noies. Hi va haver guerra; en plena batalla a la vall del Fòrum, les sabines, ja esposes i mares, es van interposar entre els seus pares i els seus marits. Es va fer la pau, i romans i sabins van formar un sol poble.
Romulus' new city had men but few women, and the neighbouring peoples wouldn't marry their daughters to those fugitives. Romulus organised games in honour of Neptune and invited the Sabines with their families. At a signal, the young Romans carried off the girls. There was war; in the thick of battle in the valley of the Forum, the Sabine women, by now wives and mothers, threw themselves between their fathers and their husbands. Peace was made, and Romans and Sabines became one people.

En temps del rei Tul·le Hostili, Roma i Alba Longa van decidir resoldre la seva guerra amb un combat: tres germans bessons per cada costat, els Horacis per Roma i els Curiacis per Alba. Van morir dos Horacis; el tercer, il·lès, va fingir fugir, i els tres Curiacis, ferits, el van perseguir separats. Els va matar un per un. En tornar, la seva germana, promesa d'un dels Curiacis, el va plorar, i Horaci la va matar. Va ser jutjat i absolt pel poble.
In the time of King Tullus Hostilius, Rome and Alba Longa decided to settle their war with a combat: three twin brothers on each side, the Horatii for Rome and the Curiatii for Alba. Two Horatii died; the third, unhurt, pretended to flee, and the three wounded Curiatii chased him one by one. He killed them in turn. On his return, his sister, betrothed to one of the Curiatii, wept for him, and Horatius killed her. He was tried and acquitted by the people.

Sext, fill de l'últim rei de Roma, Tarquini el Superb, va violar Lucrècia, esposa del seu cosí Col·latí. Ella va cridar el seu pare i el seu marit, els va explicar el que havia passat, els va fer jurar venjança i es va clavar un punyal. Luci Juni Brut va treure el punyal de la ferida i va jurar expulsar els Tarquinis. El poble es va aixecar, el rei va ser desterrat i el 509 aC Roma es va convertir en república, governada per dos cònsols elegits cada any.
Sextus, son of Rome's last king, Tarquin the Proud, raped Lucretia, wife of his cousin Collatinus. She summoned her father and her husband, told them what had happened, made them swear vengeance and stabbed herself. Lucius Junius Brutus drew the dagger from the wound and swore to drive out the Tarquins. The people rose, the king was exiled and in 509 BC Rome became a republic, governed by two consuls elected each year.

Acabats d'expulsar els reis, l'etrusc Porsenna va atacar Roma per tornar el tron a Tarquini. Horaci Cocles va defensar tot sol l'entrada del pont Sublici mentre els seus companys l'enderrocaven a la seva esquena; després es va llançar armat al Tíber i va arribar nedant a la riba. Després, el jove Muci va entrar al campament etrusc per matar Porsenna, però va matar per error el seu secretari. Capturat, va ficar la mà dreta al foc d'un altar sense fer cap gest per mostrar el valor dels romans. Porsenna, impressionat, el va deixar anar i va fer la pau. Des d'aleshores el van anomenar Escèvola, «l'esquerrà».
The kings had just been driven out when the Etruscan Porsenna attacked Rome to restore Tarquin to the throne. Horatius Cocles held the entrance to the Sublician bridge alone while his comrades demolished it behind him; then he leapt armed into the Tiber and swam to the bank. Next, the young Mucius slipped into the Etruscan camp to kill Porsenna, but killed his secretary by mistake. Captured, he thrust his right hand into an altar fire without flinching to show the courage of the Romans. Porsenna, impressed, let him go and made peace. From then on he was called Scaevola, «left-handed».
