Epictet (c. 50-c. 135), nascut a Hieràpolis de Frígia, va ser esclau d'Epafrodit, llibert de Neró, que li va permetre estudiar amb l'estoic Musoni Ruf. Ja alliberat va ensenyar a Roma i després a Nicòpolis; el seu deixeble Arrià en va recollir els ensenyaments.
Epictetus (c. 50-c. 135), born at Hierapolis in Phrygia, was the slave of Epaphroditus, Nero's freedman, who let him study with the Stoic Musonius Rufus. Lame, he taught people to distinguish what is up to us (judgements, desires) from what is not (body, property, reputation), and that freedom lies within. Once freed he taught in Rome and later at Nicopolis; his disciple Arrian recorded his teachings.
