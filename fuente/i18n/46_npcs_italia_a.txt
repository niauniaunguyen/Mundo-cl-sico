In AD 79 Vesuvius erupted. A column of pumice and ash fell for hours on Pompeii and Stabiae; then burning clouds of gas and ash swept over Herculaneum and Pompeii. Thousands of people died. Pliny the Elder died trying to rescue the people of the bay. Titus sent two former consuls as commissioners to restore Campania and put the property of those who died without heirs towards the rebuilding. The traditional date, 24 August, is disputed today: several clues point to October.

A l'Imperi es viatjava per mar en naus de càrrega, pagant passatge, dormint a coberta i portant el menjar propi. La temporada de navegació anava de la primavera a la tardor. Brundisium era el gran port cap a Orient: des d'allà es travessava en un dia a Dirraqui o a Apol·lònia, a l'Epir, i se seguia per terra per la Via Egnàtia. In the Empire people travelled by sea on cargo ships, paying for passage, sleeping on deck and bringing their own food. The sailing season ran from spring to autumn. Brundisium was the great port for the East: from there you crossed in a day to Dyrrachium or Apollonia, in Epirus, and carried on overland along the Via Egnatia. Goods travelled in amphorae, and the State hired private shipowners to carry grain to Rome.

Augustus created a state service to carry messages and officials, with posting stations along the roads, and entrusted their upkeep to curators.
