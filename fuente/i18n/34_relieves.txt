# RELIEFS: relieves y pinturas que se pueden examinar

@20fef0da
Al triangle del frontó, sobre l'entrada principal, veus Zeus assegut al centre. Al seu costat, una deessa adulta, armada amb casc i llança, sembla que acabi d'aparèixer. Hefest s'allunya amb una destral. A les cantonades, el carro del Sol surt del mar i el de la Lluna s'enfonsa.
In the triangle of the pediment, above the main entrance, you see Zeus seated in the centre. Beside him, a grown goddess, armed with helmet and spear, seems to have just appeared. Hephaestus moves away with an axe. In the corners, the Sun's chariot rises from the sea and the Moon's sinks.

Exactly. Hephaestus split open Zeus' head with an axe and out came Athena, already grown and armed. The birth happens at dawn: that's why the Sun rises in one corner and the Moon sets in the other.

In the pediment facing the Propylaia, two gods spring violently apart in the centre: a goddess with the aegis and a bearded god with a trident. Behind each, a chariot. Between them an olive tree appears.

High up on the walls, in the shade of the colonnade, a continuous band of reliefs shows horsemen, chariots, musicians, jar-bearers and animals for sacrifice. All move towards the east façade, where the gods, seated, await them.

Aquesta és la interpretació tradicional: la processó de les Panatenees, amb el lliurament del peple a Atena. Alguns estudiosos proposen una altra lectura, el sacrifici de les filles del rei Erecteu. És un debat obert.

A famous relief of this scene, known from Roman copies, may come from an altar in the Agora; the attribution is debated.
