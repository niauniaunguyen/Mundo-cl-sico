Gai Valeri Catul (c. 84-54 aC), nascut a Verona, va ser el més famós dels «poetes nous», que a Roma van imitar els poetes grecs d'Alexandria: poemes breus, cultes i molt treballats. Gaius Valerius Catullus (c. 84-54 BC), born in Verona, was the most famous of the "new poets", who in Rome imitated the Greek poets of Alexandria: short, learned, highly polished poems. His 116 poems speak of love (for Lesbia, probably Clodia), friendship, hatred and mockery of politicians such as Caesar. He influenced Virgil, Horace, Ovid and Martial.

En llicenciar-se després de vint anys o més de servei, els legionaris rebien terres o diners. August va fundar moltes colònies per als seus veterans a Itàlia i a les províncies, com Emerita Augusta (25 aC), per als soldats de les guerres contra càntabres i àsturs. Els colons rebien lots mesurats pels agrimensors, i la ciutat es construïa segons un model romà: fòrum, temples, teatre, amfiteatre. Així es van estendre la llengua llatina i els costums romans per l'Imperi.
On discharge after twenty years or more of service, legionaries received land or money. Augustus founded many colonies for his veterans in Italy and in the provinces, such as Emerita Augusta (25 BC), for the soldiers of the wars against the Cantabri and Astures. The colonists received plots measured by the surveyors, and the town was built on a Roman model: forum, temples, theatre, amphitheatre. In this way the Latin language and Roman customs spread across the Empire.
